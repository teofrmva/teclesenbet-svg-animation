4. Fade-Up Entrance

Reflection

The hardest animation for me was the self-drawing line because I had to understand how stroke-dasharray and stroke-dashoffset work together. I learned that stroke-dasharray controls the visible and hidden parts of a line, while stroke-dashoffset changes where the dash starts. By animating the offset, I could make the line look like it was drawing itself. I have seen hover transitions on shopping websites when buttons or product images change as the mouse moves over them. I have seen spinning loaders on YouTube when videos are buffering. Self-drawing lines appear in animated logos and website introductions. Fade-up animations are common on modern websites when text and images appear as a page loads. This assignment helped me understand how CSS can make SVG graphics more interactive without JavaScript. I also learned why reduced-motion settings are important for accessibility.
